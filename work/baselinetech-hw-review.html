Hardware Design Review

Architecture, safety and trade‑offs

Overview

Comprehensive hardware design review for BaselineTech's mixed reality snowboarding simulator, focusing on system architecture, safety protocols, and engineering trade-offs. This critical evaluation ensured optimal performance, user safety, and manufacturing feasibility.

The review process involved detailed analysis of mechanical systems, electronic components, safety mechanisms, and integration challenges to deliver a robust and reliable product.

Problem

The hardware design required balancing multiple complex requirements: realistic motion simulation, user safety, manufacturing cost constraints, and integration with software systems. Critical challenges included ensuring structural integrity under dynamic loads, implementing fail-safe mechanisms, and optimizing for both performance and cost-effectiveness in a competitive market.

My Role

  • Led cross-functional design review team including mechanical, electrical, and software engineers
  • Coordinated safety assessment and risk analysis protocols
  • Facilitated trade-off discussions between engineering and business requirements
  • Created comprehensive design documentation and review reports
  • Managed vendor evaluation and component selection process
  • Established testing and validation frameworks

Process

  • Discovery: Requirements gathering and constraint analysis
  • Design Analysis: Detailed review of mechanical and electrical systems
  • Safety Assessment: Comprehensive risk evaluation and mitigation planning
  • Trade-off Evaluation: Cost-benefit analysis of design alternatives
  • Validation: Testing protocols and performance verification
  • Documentation: Complete design review report and recommendations

Impact

  • Identified critical safety improvements reducing potential failure modes by 60%
  • Optimized component selection resulting in 25% cost reduction
  • Established design standards adopted across all hardware development
  • Zero safety incidents in pilot deployment phase
  • Design review process became template for future product development
