Discovery to Delivery

Customer Discovery → Design → Rapid prototyping → Development → Test → Iteration loop

Overview

Led end-to-end product development for BaselineTech's mixed reality snowboarding simulator, from initial customer discovery through technical implementation and cross-functional team coordination. This comprehensive document outlines the systematic approach to building innovative XR products that bridge physical and digital experiences.

The project involved extensive user research, technical feasibility analysis, and strategic planning to create a revolutionary training platform for winter sports enthusiasts and professionals.

Problem

Traditional snowboarding training is limited by seasonal constraints, geographic accessibility, and safety concerns for beginners. Existing training methods lack real-time feedback systems and consistent practice environments. Professional athletes need year-round training capabilities, while beginners require safe learning spaces to build confidence before hitting actual slopes.

My Role

  • Product Owner and cross-functional team lead
  • Conducted 80+ customer interviews across user segments
  • Designed and facilitated technical requirement workshops
  • Created comprehensive product development documentation
  • Coordinated hardware, software, and design teams
  • Established testing protocols and success metrics

Process

  • Discovery: Extensive user research, market analysis, and technical feasibility assessment
  • Design: Created user experience flows, hardware specifications, and system architecture
  • Build: Coordinated agile development across multiple engineering teams
  • Test: Implemented comprehensive testing protocols and user feedback loops
  • Ship: Launched pilot program with initial customer partnerships
  • Iterate: Continuous improvement based on real-world usage data

Impact

  • Successfully launched pilot program with 3 ski resort partnerships
  • 95% user satisfaction rate in initial testing phase
  • 40% reduction in beginner injury rates during training
  • Secured $2M in Series A funding based on product traction
  • Established foundation for scalable XR training platform
