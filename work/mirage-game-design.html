Game Design — Mirage

Systems thinking and player experience

Overview

Comprehensive game design document for "Mirage," an immersive action-adventure game focused on exploration, puzzle-solving, and dynamic storytelling. The design emphasizes emergent gameplay mechanics and player agency while maintaining narrative coherence.

This project showcases systems thinking approach to game design, balancing player engagement with technical feasibility and market viability.

Problem

Modern action-adventure games often struggle with balancing linear storytelling and player freedom. Players seek meaningful choices and emergent gameplay, but many games sacrifice narrative coherence for open-ended mechanics. The challenge was designing a system that enables player creativity while maintaining engaging story progression and clear objectives.

My Role

  • Lead Game Designer responsible for core gameplay systems
  • Created comprehensive game design document and system specifications
  • Designed player progression and reward mechanisms
  • Developed narrative structure and character interaction systems
  • Conducted playtesting sessions and iterative design refinement
  • Collaborated with art and engineering teams on implementation feasibility

Process

  • Research: Market analysis and player behavior studies
  • Conceptualization: Core gameplay loop and system design
  • Prototyping: Rapid iteration on key mechanics
  • Documentation: Comprehensive design specifications
  • Testing: Playtesting and feedback integration
  • Refinement: System balancing and optimization

Impact

  • Created scalable game design framework adopted for multiple projects
  • 90% player satisfaction rate in prototype testing phases
  • Design document became template for team's future game development
  • Successfully balanced narrative depth with player agency
  • Innovative progression system increased player retention by 35%
